# My Daily Compass

Build a comprehensive personal life-tracking and fitness web/mobile application for my exclusive personal use. The app should feature a clean, modern, dark-mode-friendly UI. 

Here are the core modules and features required:

1. Daily Routine & Time Tracker:

- Track daily blocks for School/College time, Study/Reading time, and Gym workouts.

- Daily logging dashboard to input hours spent and check off routine tasks.

2. Health, Nutrition & Hydration Tracker:

- Water Tracker: Quick-add buttons to log glasses/liters of water consumed daily against a goal.

- Food & Diet Log: A meal tracker (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks) where I can input what I eat.

- Smart Food Suggestion Engine: If a logged meal lacks necessary nutrients or macros (e.g., low protein or missing vegetables), the app should flag it and suggest a quick alternative or food improvisation to fix it.

3. Gym Exercise Tracker & Video Library:

- Workout logger to track exercises, sets, reps, and weights.

- Built-in exercise library database containing demonstration videos (or embedded GIF/video links) for standard gym movements (e.g., bench press, squats, deadlifts) so I can verify proper form.

4. Growth & Analytics Reports (Weekly, Monthly, 6-Months, Yearly):

- Aggregate historical data to generate clean graphs and summary reports.

- Reports should show trends in: total study/reading hours, academic attendance vs. college time, water intake consistency, and gym progress (weight lifted/volume over time).

5. Data & Storage:

- Set up a simple local database (like SQLite or Supabase) so all my historical entries persist safely.
